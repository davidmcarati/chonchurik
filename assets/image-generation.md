# Johnny Silverfly artwork

Two decorative assets, neither of them a trading result.

`assets/johnny-silverfly.jpg` is the README hero and the project's face.
`assets/stonkfly.png` is the pixel-art card it replaced. The card is kept because
the name is kept: the Python package, the `python -m stonkfly` entry point and the
`STONKFLY_*` variables are all still called that, and Johnny is son of Stonkfly.
It is no longer shown in the README.

## Johnny Silverfly

Mascot portrait, `assets/johnny-silverfly.jpg`, 1122x1402, JPEG q92 without
chroma subsampling. A photographic render rather than pixel art, so PNG cost
4.4 MB here for no visible gain.

Decorative only. The ticker board, the percentages and the slogans in the scene
are invented; nothing in it is a trading result, a screenshot or a claim. The
README's opening paragraph says so, directly under the image, and that
sentence must survive any edit to it.
